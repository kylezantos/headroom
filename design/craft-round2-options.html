Motion and details, as a decision each

This round is opportunities, not bugs. Nothing below crashes or lies to you — it is the layer above that: moments that hard-cut where they could dissolve, a severity ramp that stops encoding severity, four names for one act. The pre-ship audit cleared the blocking defects; this pass decides how the thing feels.

The weighting is Emil first (restraint, and the frequency gate — a thing you see thirty times a day earns less motion than a thing you see twice), Jakub second (polish, subtler exits), and Apple's fluid-interface rules for springs and Reduce Motion. Standing rules held throughout: nothing loops or pulses, nothing exceeds 300ms, one cause gets one motion, and Reduce Motion keeps crossfade timing while movement becomes instant (S2A). No new motion token is proposed anywhere in this round — every motion item reuses settle, quickSettle, pressDown, hoverIn/hoverOut, valueSpring, reorder, pressScale.

Before you start

How to read these, and what nobody could verify

Craft areas. Live capture panels are real crops of the PNGs in .context/qc/craft-round2/live/ and settings-harness/, embedded at 2× and rendered at half width, so 2px of image = 1pt of app. Reconstructed from code panels are HTML mocks of a state nobody captured — hover, press, an in-flight poll, a tooltip, an open menu — with every value read out of the file cited on the panel. Mock panels are always HTML at 1px = 1pt, framed to match the crop beside them so your eye can flick between the two.

Motion areas. Both panels are mocks, because a screenshot cannot show a hard cut. One Play control drives Current and Proposed together, so the difference is the only thing that moves. Curves and durations are the real tokens read from Sources/HeadroomApp/UI/HeadroomMotion.swift: settle 200ms, quickSettle 120ms and pressDown 100ms on cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1); hoverOut a zero-bounce spring at response 0.18; valueSpring response 0.30 ζ 1.0; reorder response 0.35 ζ 1.0; pressScale 0.96. Springs are integrated on this page from SwiftUI's own definition (mass 1, k = (2π/response)², c = 4π·ζ/response), so they retarget from position and velocity the way the real ones do.

The two global controls reach every demo. Reduce Motion in the top bar switches every demo to the S2A behaviour — crossfades keep their timing, movement becomes instant. 0.25× beside each Play slows that demo four-fold without changing its shape, which is the only honest way to judge a 120ms fill.

Nothing on this page pulses, loops, autoplays, or animates on scroll, including the page chrome itself. If something is moving, you pressed Play.

Not verified — read this before you trust a number

  • No motion was observed at runtime. Every timing claim is read from the HeadroomMotion tokens and their call sites, not watched. The demos are this page's reconstruction of what that code says will happen.
  • Reduce Motion on/off is your check. Agents do not change accessibility settings, so M7's nine drift sites were confirmed in source only.
  • C4 and C18's contrast numbers were computed by the reviewer with the repo's own APCA helper and OKLCH conversion; Fable did not recompute them. Every colour proposal wants a live --demo capture in both appearances before it lands.
  • Hover and press bands cannot be captured — M6, M10 and C21 need one live pointer pass. BUGS.md already tracks the HoverButtonStyle hover-band question.
  • .unavailable windows and ProviderSetupRecoveryCard have no fixture in this round, so the two B2 rows about them are reasoned, not seen.
  • Harness renders are geometry and copy evidence only (the Xcode 26.5 appearance-capture bug), so colour is never read off them — colour findings use the live MenuBarExtra captures.
  • Four motion areas carry no reviewer brief.
