O15, the sentence is the button: twelve variations

Round 2 of on-card-secondary-button-options.html. Every variation keeps O15's idea: the status sentence is the click target and the separate button row disappears. What changes is the chrome, the hint that it's clickable, the alignment, and how the secondary action is handled.

Each card shows the three states the row has to handle. Personal is signed out (Sign In + Check Now). Work is waiting on Terminal (Stop Waiting only, and the line isn't clickable). Team has a two-line failure (Try Again + Check Now). Side is a healthy account, for judging weight against live data. V0 is O15 exactly as drawn in round 1.

One rule holds across all of them: ↗ always means "hands off to Terminal," which covers both Sign In and Try Again. Check Now moves to a refresh glyph, Stop Waiting to ✕.
