How to read these

Current panels marked Live capture are real crops of the PNGs in .context/qc/preship-audit/, embedded at 2× and rendered at half width, so 2px of image = 1pt of app. The tightest crop that shows the defect is used — one card or one row, not a whole popover.

Current panels marked Reconstructed from code are HTML mocks, not screenshots: that state has no capture (the "Add Key" pending row, the menu-bar item itself, the Sparkle notes dialog). Every value in them is read out of the file cited on the panel — judge placement and copy from them, not letterform detail.

Proposed panels are always HTML mocks at 1px = 1pt, framed to match the crop beside them so your eye can flick between the two. Colours and sizes come from the real tokens (BrandColorValues.swift, MeterStyle.swift, the cited Sources/HeadroomApp/UI/*.swift); anything worked out on this page is labelled derived.

Appearance follows the finding. A finding about light mode is shown on a light stage, a dark one on a dark stage — the same appearance the capture was taken in, so the comparison is fair. Two brand-mark stand-ins (exclamationmark.triangle.fill, the mini spinner) are hand-drawn approximations at the right size and ink.

Nothing animates. No pulse, no loop, no autoplay, no transition longer than a hover tint. The waiting spinner is drawn at rest.
