Refresh spinner, halftone directions

The website's sky is a halftone: one fixed grid of dots where the amount of ink is carried by each dot's size and brightness. The moon's lit side is fat dots, its dark side is tiny "earthshine" specks, and its halo shrinks and fades with distance. Flicker (and our #148 arc) is a flip-dot instead: every dot is either on or off, the same size. That's probably why the old spinner felt close but not right. These ten use the site's own constants (dot fill 0.42, size 30–100% of max, halo brightness falling off as f²) so they share its texture.

Every one sits where the native spinner sits today: laid over the Refresh bezel, whose width is kept by the hidden label (PopoverView.swift:625). That slot is about 46×15pt inside a 68×24pt bezel, so ideas come in two shapes: square at 15pt, or a strip at 44×13 that uses the bezel's full width (halftone needs room; the strip gives it the most). All are neutral ink, all travel rather than pulse, and each holds a still frame under Reduce Motion.

Round 4: the other loading moments

Headroom has nine other places where something is loading or waiting. These ideas extend the Reprint family to them with one small vocabulary, so every wait in the app reads the same way:

Slosh = Headroom is workingA surface travelling right, on the menu bar's clock. Used whenever the app itself is fetching.
Drip = waiting on youA slow, single drop into an empty vessel. Used when the next move is yours (Terminal, browser), so it never looks like the app is busy.
Ghost → print = old or unknown → freshFaint halftone means "not current yet"; ink printing in from the bottom or left means "this just arrived".

Where dots blur (anything 11pt or smaller), the ideas use the solid vector vessel from the menu bar instead of halftone. Widgets can't run looping animation, so their idea is a still.

If I had to pick

  1. 06 Tide: the same motion as the menu-bar slosh, so the pill and the button tell one story during a refresh. The strip gives the halftone the most dots, so it reads best at real size.
  2. 02 Day Arc: the closest quote of the website (the dotted arc, a body with a halftone halo, "watch a day go by"). Most on-brand, a little more illustrative.
  3. 05 Radar: the safest square one. It reads instantly as "working", and the fading trail is halftone doing what it does best.

Left out on purpose: ripple rings from a centre dot (throbbing rings), a breathing moon halo, a three-dot typing ellipsis, and twinkling stars. All four are scale or opacity pulses in place, which the no-pulse rule rules out. Each idea above moves something across the grid instead.
