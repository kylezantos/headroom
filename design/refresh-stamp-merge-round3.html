1 · Stamp merge, three ways, cleaned up

You said: "I like the concept of the options in version 13, all three of them, but the spacing doesn't look quite right… they look custom but unfinished… make it look more well-crafted and intentional, but still fitting into our design language."
  • What was off: the status text stayed where the stamp sits at rest, so it ended up 35pt from the spinner inside the bar. The bar's right end was a guessed width. V13b's fill drew a hard-edged second shade over the button area, and V13c's field started 3pt from Refresh, closer than any other gap in the footer.
  • What changed: while a refresh runs, the text moves into the container and sits 7pt after the spinner. The text changes words at the same moment, so the move reads as a handover rather than a jump.

2 · Menu-bar slosh on every refresh

You said: "I'm pretty sure that I want to do the liquid sloshing in the pills in the menu bar. And it should do that every time it's refreshed, whether or not the user clicks the refresh button."
  • Decided, with one question left: which pills move during a background poll? Headroom doesn't refresh everything at once in the background. Each account has its own timer, so there's a poll going on somewhere much more often than every five minutes.
  • All pills, every poll: the whole shelf moves whenever any account is being checked. With 10 accounts that's roughly 10–15% of the time, and the scheduler allows a lane to be busy up to half the time.
  • Only the pill being checked: each pill shows its provider's main account, so a pill sloshes only while that account is being checked. That's about 4s every 5 minutes per pill, around 1% of the time, and the motion always means "this number is updating now". I'd pick this one.
  • Try both below. "Background poll" checks one account the way the timer does, so the Refresh button doesn't change. "Refresh all" is you pressing Refresh.
