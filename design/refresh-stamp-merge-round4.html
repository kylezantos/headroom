Stamp merge: ten more directions

Ten variations on V13's idea, that while a refresh runs, Refresh and the freshness stamp become one status. They range from the most restrained to one or two playful ones. Each shows three frozen states (at rest, mid-refresh, finished) and a live copy you can click. Everything keeps the round-3 rules: 10pt gaps, 24pt height and 7pt radius for anything in the footer, 11pt status text, and neutral ink only.

How these were generatedUsing the design-motion-principles lenses, weighted for a utility you open many times a day. Emil (restraint, speed: should this animate at all?) leads, Jakub (production polish) is second, and Jhey (play: what could this become?) only drives W8 and W9. Each card is tagged with the lens that shaped it.
What actually differsWhere the status lives (inside Refresh, beside it, above it, in the text itself), whether the geometry changes at all, and how progress reads: count, hairline, account marks, meter dots, ink, or liquid. W2, W4 and W8 move no geometry; W1, W3, W6, W7 and W9 reshape the button; W5 and W10 add a surface.

Where I'd start

  1. W1 Clip reveal. It keeps everything you liked about V13 and removes what felt off: no text travels and nothing stretches, because the bar is revealed rather than grown. It's also buildable on the native bezel with a mask, so there's no PA11b risk.
  2. W5 Mark stream. The most informative at a glance, and built entirely from native parts.
  3. W7 Orb and bare text. The most restrained: the chrome shrinks and the words carry the state.
  4. W2 is the boldest: it removes the merge problem by merging permanently. It needs your sign-off on a button labelled with a time.
