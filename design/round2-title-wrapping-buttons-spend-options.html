How to read these

1px = 1pt. Popover 384 wide, 19 inset, radius 22 (PopoverView.swift:153-157). Card padding 13, radius 10 (ProviderCardView.swift:109-111). Identity content axis: 15pt mark + 6pt gap = 21pt inset on both sides (ProviderCardIdentityRows.swift:6-15). Meter row typography from UsageBarView.swift:20-26.

Every colour and size is a real token with a file:line, or is labelled derived where I had to pick a light-mode counterpart that does not exist in the tree yet.

Browser line breaking is not SwiftUI's. The W2 specimens use -apple-system at the real point sizes and the real container widths, so the breaks are close — but SwiftUI's Text lays out through TextKit with its own hyphenation and push-out behaviour. Treat the specimens as a very good approximation, not as a screenshot of the app.

The native button is drawn, not real. AppKit's .bordered .controlSize(.small) cannot be reproduced in a browser. Everywhere it appears below it is a 20pt-tall rounded rect at radius 5 with the system button gradient, and it is labelled as an approximation.

Meters. T1 uses Micro · Segmented (5 cells, 6×5, gap 2, r1.5 — MeterStyle.swift:102-104) with a flat severity fill, to match your Paper frame. The shipped default is micro.chip.event, where a healthy bar is neutral grey, not green. Everywhere else on this page uses that default.
