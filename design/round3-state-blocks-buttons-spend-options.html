State blocks, buttons, spend rows

You said the error messages and pills are awkwardly placed, the "Sign in with…" line should drop its icon and line up with the text above it, buttons should stop at the same padding the marks start at, every button should be the thin "Check now" kind, the title grey might be too dark, and the cards with error text just don't look designed yet. All of that is true, and it is all one problem: a card that is doing fine has one set of rules, and a card that needs your attention has another. This page turns that into six decisions.

Four principles do most of the work below. Shared edges — one leading edge for marks, one for text, one trailing edge for numerals and controls. Group with space, 2× rule — the gap between two groups is at least twice the gap inside one, so grouping is visible without a line or a box. Controls are distinct from content, and content is never shaped like a control. Name leads, metadata follows, actions trail.

How to read these

1px = 1pt. Popover 384 wide, 19 inset, radius 22 (PopoverView.swift:176-177). Card 346 wide, padding 13, radius 10 (ProviderCardView.swift:109, APISpendSectionView.swift:48). Content axis: 13 card padding + 15 mark slot + 6 spacing = 34pt from either card edge (ProviderCardIdentityRows.swift:6-15). Numerals are 18pt semibold with monospaced digits (UsageBarView.swift:20-26).

Meters are Micro · Segmented throughout — 5 cells, 6×5, gap 2, r1.5 (MeterStyle.swift:102-104) — because that is what your screenshot shows. Lit cells behind the head cell are stepped down by the carried --ramp-amt token, which is how the darker leading cells in your shot read.

Every colour and size is a real token with a file:line, or is labelled derived where a value had to be worked out on this page (the G1 light counterparts, the exact vertical padding that lands a tag on 18pt, and the two SF Symbols that are drawn approximations rather than the real glyphs).

Two symbols are drawn, not real. exclamationmark.triangle.fill and person.crop.circle.badge.exclamationmark are hand-drawn stand-ins at the right size and ink. Judge placement and weight from them, not letterform detail.

Nothing animates. The waiting spinner in S2A is drawn at rest as a static ring. There is no loop, no pulse and no autoplay anywhere on this page.
