What's wrong with the widgets today

They're a generic progress-bar list, and the popover no longer is.

  1. No single answer. Every number is the same size, so nothing tells you at a glance whether you're fine. The popover's big right-aligned numeral column does that job; the widgets bury it beside a long bar.
  2. Full-width bars the popover dropped. The popover moved to compact gauges (segmented cells, chip, tube, vessel). The widgets still use edge-to-edge bars, which read as a settings slider, not an instrument.
  3. Colour looks random. With colour only when low, one amber bar among three grey ones looks like a bug, not a warning. Your own popover runs Always colored, and the widgets disagree with it.
  4. Dead space and cramped space together. The large tile leaves roughly a third of its height empty. The medium tile squeezes two columns until each bar is a stub.
  5. Nothing that says Headroom. The vessel (the app icon and every menu-bar pill) never appears. The widget could come from any usage app.
  6. Labels that don't earn their space. "Weekly · 3h", "left" printed six times, and a floating "Updated 4 min ago" add ink without adding meaning.
Where I’d start (★ picks): 02 Vessel Hero or 15 Liquid Level for small, because they bring back the Headroom vessel and give one answer at a glance. 19 Nested Meter for medium: calm, compact, and it shows how the 5-hour window sits inside the week. 12 Tightest First for large: a triage list that can’t hide a nearly empty window. 08 Batteries is the safe, fully native fallback.
