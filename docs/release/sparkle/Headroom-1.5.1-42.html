More room for every account

  • A compact account layout. Choose Standard or Compact in Settings › Appearance. Compact keeps each account on one line, while accounts that need attention keep their recovery controls.
  • Two columns when you need them. Larger account lists use two columns, and the popover returns to one when the list fits.
  • A softer popover edge. The rounded glass has a matching shadow, without the extra tight corner around it.
  • Automatic updates now work. Turn on “Download and install updates automatically” in Settings › General, and Headroom installs new versions when you quit. It stays off until you choose it.
  • General and About together. Version, support and maker links now live in one General pane. The sidebar shows when an update is ready, with a Restart to Update button.
  • Roomier Liquid Level widget. Its columns sit further from the divider and corners.
  • A cleaner installer. The install window keeps its icons in place, even with hidden files shown in Finder.
