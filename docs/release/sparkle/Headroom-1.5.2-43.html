Your accounts, the way you recognize them

  • Use email addresses as account names. Turn on “Use email addresses as account names” in Settings › Accounts to identify every account by its email. Saved names return when you turn it off, and remain the fallback when an email is unavailable.
  • Choose how long account lists fit. In Settings › Appearance, choose “Expand horizontally” for a second column or “Scroll vertically” to keep one scrolling column. Both work with Standard and Compact; horizontal expansion stays the default.
  • Clearer layout choices. Visual previews illustrate both directions, and Compact is recommended for 5+ accounts. Compact email names keep the start of the address visible, with the full address available on hover.
