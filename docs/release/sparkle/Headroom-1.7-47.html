Follow your account switches

  • Headroom follows your CLI’s current login. Switch accounts through the official CLI or an account switcher that updates its login and account record together, and Headroom picks up the change. Names, readings and reminders stay with the account they belong to.
  • Keep the accounts you switch away from. Claude and Codex accounts stay visible with their last reading and a “Last read” label. Once a reset passes, Headroom hides the old number until that account is read again. Switch back and its name and history return.
  • One account, one row. When your default Claude or Codex login matches an account you’ve added to Headroom, they show as one row. Neither login is removed, and reminders already sent for a window aren’t repeated when the rows join or separate.
  • The same account names on your iPhone. The companion now receives the names shown on your Mac, including email addresses when “Use email addresses as account names” is on.

Login changes are detected for all five providers. Remembered accounts and matching-account rows are available for Claude and Codex, whose CLIs provide stable account identifiers. Widgets continue to show each provider’s main account.
