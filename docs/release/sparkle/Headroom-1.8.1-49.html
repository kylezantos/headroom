More reliable Antigravity checks

  • No surprise sign-in tabs. Antigravity usage checks no longer open browser sign-in pages when agy is signed out.
  • Refresh after signing in. After signing back into agy in Terminal, click Refresh to resume Antigravity readings. Routine checks keep waiting while you are signed out.
  • Popover fits your display. With Scroll vertically selected, the account list grows to fit the available screen height, up to 900 points, then scrolls. On smaller displays, the footer stays within reach.

Thanks to Matt Van Horn (@mvanhorn) for the Antigravity fix.
